- Three words (2026-11-06): **clair, direct, fiable** (Franck's pick).
- Not (2026-11-06): **luxe, prétentieux** (Franck's pick). So: no gold, no "d'exception" talk, no velvet serif.
- Palette (2026-11-06, Franck: "keep, that's super clean and elegant"): direction 1 "Papier et Bleu France", preview https://claude.ai/artifact/NFrZGLSc59X79SV3bx68Ye

  - Encre #161616: text; muted #4A4A4A
  - Bleu France #000091: names, buttons, links (the only action colour)
  - Bleu clair #ECECFE (DSFR blue-france 950): labels, selected row

  - Font: Public Sans (Google Fonts) 400/600/800
  - Risks named on the preview, kept by Franck: State look ("administratif"), blue carries most, Public Sans neutral.
